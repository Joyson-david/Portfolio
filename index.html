Case 01 / Marketing operations

Marketing Operations Agent.

GumloopClaudeHubSpotGmailSheetsGA
Agent architecture · six workflows in a loop

Problem.

Marketing operations usually lives as a campaign calendar and a pile of manual CRM clean-up. Leads land with bad data, scoring is inconsistent, nurture emails hit the wrong contacts and reporting is rebuilt by hand every week.

Build.

A custom Gumloop agent that treats marketing operations as a revenue system: capture, hygiene, qualify, nurture, measure, govern. The bottom arrow is the maintenance loop. Alerts and scheduled reports send new work back into capture.

Six workflows.

  1. 01Capture — Schema, sources, UTMs. Inspects HubSpot properties before creating new ones. Forms and event lists land here.
  2. 02Hygiene — Validate, dedupe, fail path. Role inboxes and bad emails never write to the CRM.
  3. 03Qualify — Fit score plus behaviour score, MQL threshold, owner assignment, 24-hour sales-task SLA.
  4. 04Nurture — Language journeys, MQL suppression, Day-0 notes. No email to synthetic contacts.
  5. 05Measure — Funnel snapshot, data-gap flags, Gmail draft report (never auto-sent).
  6. 06Govern — Metric dictionary, alerts, schedules. One-off work becomes a system only after it has run once.

Case 02 / Marketing analytics

Data Analyst Agent.

GumloopClaudeGoogle AnalyticsHubSpotSheetsGitHub
Agent architecture · five phases, phase 5 feeds phase 1

Problem.

Business questions about funnel, churn, campaigns and CRM usually get a generic chat answer or a one-off spreadsheet nobody refreshes. The data lives in five tools and the analysis never becomes a system.

Build.

A custom Gumloop agent that turns a business question into sourced, cleaned, analysed, visualised and monitored insight against live connected data. Phase 5 feeds back into phase 1, so one-off analyses become recurring views.

Starter jobs
Why did this KPI move?Profile and clean this datasetBuild a recurring ops viewSegment and cohort cut

Five phases.

  1. 01Ingestion — Requirement mapping (question to metrics, grain, time window, filters); source identification across GA, HubSpot, Sheets, Drive and GitHub; extraction with row counts and time range logged.
  2. 02Hygiene — Audit and profiling, then cleaning (dates in IST, nulls documented), then feature engineering (CLV, MAU, conversion, staging tables).
  3. 03Analysis — EDA, cohort and segment cuts, root cause (volume vs rate, traffic vs conversion). Hypothesis is labelled separately from evidence.
  4. 04Translation — Dashboards and files, narrative memos, a named next owner (product, marketing or exec).
  5. 05Monitoring — Email alerts, scheduled refreshes, a metric dictionary with every recurring output.
